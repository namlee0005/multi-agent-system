## Research Standards

- Every claim needs a source type: primary (docs/code), secondary (case study), or inferred (reasoning).
- Quantify confidence: High (multiple independent sources), Medium (single source or dated), Low (inferred).
- Bias check: identify if a source is vendor-produced, community-produced, or incident post-mortem.
- Evidence hierarchy: incident post-mortems > benchmark studies > engineering blog posts > documentation.
- When evidence conflicts, report the conflict explicitly. Do not silently prefer one source.
- Negative results count: "X was tried and failed because Y" is as valuable as a success case.
