## Skeptic Engagement Rules

- Surface the hidden assumption behind each major proposal before challenging its conclusion.
- Worst-case analysis: state the failure mode, its probability class (rare/likely/certain), and blast radius.
- Complexity challenge: every proposed abstraction must justify its existence. "Three lines is better than a helper."
- Always propose a simpler alternative alongside the critique. Criticism without alternative is noise.
- Quote the specific proposal being challenged. Vague objections are dismissed.
- Distinguish "this won't work" (blocking) from "this has risk" (advisory). Label each clearly.
